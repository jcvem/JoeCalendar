# JoeCalendar Privacy Policy

*Draft — Last updated: August 2026*

**VEM Studio** ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how **JoeCalendar** collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service").

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Calendar Data
- **Device Calendars (Apple EventKit)**: With your explicit permission, JoeCalendar accesses your iOS Calendar events to display and sync them locally on your device. We do not upload your private device calendars to our servers unless you explicitly share an event with a JoeCalendar group circle.
- **Google Calendar**: If you choose to connect your Google Account, we access your Google Calendar events via official Google APIs solely to synchronize schedules within JoeCalendar.
- **JoeCalendar Events**: Events, circles, friend groups, notes, and reminders created directly within JoeCalendar are stored securely in our cloud database (Firebase Firestore).

### 1.2 Account & Authentication Information
- **Third-Party Sign-In**: When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your basic profile identifiers (e.g., user ID, name, email address as provided by the identity provider).
- **Email Sign-In**: When you register via email, we collect your email address and authentication credentials.
- **Guest Mode**: If you use Guest Mode, an anonymous identifier is assigned to your device session without collecting personally identifiable information.

### 1.3 Usage & Diagnostic Data
- App interaction metrics (such as feature usage and crash reports) may be collected in aggregate, anonymized form to diagnose technical issues and improve app stability.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for the following purposes:
- **Service Provision**: To display, create, edit, and synchronize your calendar schedules across your devices and with friends/groups you designate.
- **Authentication & Security**: To verify your identity, secure your account, and prevent fraudulent activity.
- **Customer Support**: To respond to your inquiries, bug reports, and feedback.
- **Product Improvement**: To analyze app performance and enhance the user experience.

We do **not** sell your personal data or calendar details to third parties or advertisers.

---

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing & Data Protection Compliance

We process your personal information in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the **Taiwan Personal Data Protection Act (個人資料保護法, PDPA)** and principles of the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**:
- **Consent**: When you grant permissions (e.g., calendar access, sign-in authorization).
- **Contractual Necessity**: To provide you with the calendar services you requested.
- **Legitimate Interests**: To secure and optimize the performance of our application.

---

## 4. Third-Party Service Providers

We utilize trusted third-party cloud infrastructure and SDKs to provide the Service:
- **Google Cloud / Firebase**: Authentication, Firestore cloud database, and Cloud Functions hosting in secure data centers.
- **Apple Inc.**: App Store in-app purchases, StoreKit subscription verification, Apple Sign-In, and EventKit APIs.
- **Google LLC**: Google Sign-In and Google Calendar API services.

These third parties process data on our behalf in compliance with their respective privacy and security standards.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Security

- **Retention**: We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with the Service. If you delete your account, your personal information and cloud-stored events will be deleted or anonymized within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.
- **Security**: We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards (including HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest) to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

---

## 6. Your Rights & Choices

Under applicable data protection laws (including the Taiwan PDPA and GDPR), you have the right to:
1. **Access & Inquire**: Request details on the personal data we hold about you.
2. **Obtain Copies**: Request a copy of your stored personal information.
3. **Correction & Supplementation**: Correct or update inaccurate or incomplete information.
4. **Cessation of Processing**: Request that we cease collecting, processing, or using your personal data.
5. **Deletion (Erasure)**: Request the deletion of your account and associated personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, you can use the in-app account management tools or email us directly at [jc@vem-studio.com](mailto:jc@vem-studio.com).

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

JoeCalendar is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data without parental consent, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to remove such information.
